Usage

shader, drag-effect, reveal-effect are independent. Turn on the effects you want and set the others to none. The browser clips the effects to the shape of the letters.

<!-- Plain text with native selection -->
<shader-font shader="none" drag-effect="none">
  Plain text
</shader-font>
<!-- Reveal for 1.8 seconds, then repeat after a 5-second pause -->
<shader-font shader="none" drag-effect="none"
  reveal-effect="burn" reveal-ms="1800"
  reveal-interval-ms="5000">
  Letters appear along a burning edge
</shader-font>
<!-- Hologram with elastic dragging -->
<shader-font shader="hologram" drag-effect="elastic">
  Pull this text
</shader-font>
